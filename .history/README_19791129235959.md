# Raffles Residences Boston — Resident Intranet

A private online home for residents of Raffles Residences Boston: reserve amenities,
request the concierge, pay condo fees, meet neighbours, and take part in building
governance — all in one place.

> **Demo site only.** Everything shown is simulated. No real residents, statements,
> or bookings are involved.

## What you can do

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A quiet landing page that leads you into each part of the residence. |
| **Amenities** | Photos, descriptions and reservation requests for the Residents' Lounge (Floor 21), private dining, spa and more — with catering options and current bookings. |
| **Concierge** | Submit a service request and follow its status. |
| **Events** | RSVP to resident events and suggest new ones. |
| **Account** | View monthly statements, pay condo fees, and see your payment history. |
| **Services** | Valet the car, report a maintenance issue, and track package notifications. |
| **Directory** | Your resident profile with opt-in visibility, plus neighbours who choose to be listed. |
| **Messages** | Private and group conversations between residents. |
| **Community** | Interest groups, discussion and a social feed. |
| **Marketplace** | Buy, sell, give away, or ask neighbours for recommendations. |
| **Proposals** | Resident ideas that everyone can vote up or down. |
| **Governance** | Board measures, live ballots and the governing documents. |
| **Management** | Board and staff photos and bios, announcements, and the monthly satisfaction survey. |

## Signing in

Most sections require a resident sign-in. On the demo, use any listed resident email
with the passcode **`raffles2026`**. Accounts are issued by the Residences Office —
there is no public sign-up.

## Accessibility

The interface is built to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-navigable throughout, visible focus
outlines, screen-reader labels, and contrast-checked colour throughout.
